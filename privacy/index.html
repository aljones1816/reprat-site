Rep Rat

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Rep Rat ("we", "our", or "us") is a workout tracking app built for iOS. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is handled.

1. Data You Create

Rep Rat stores the following data you enter in the app:

  • Workout sessions, exercises, sets, reps, and weights
  • Workout templates and routines
  • Bodyweight entries and weight goals
  • Personal records (PRs)
  • App settings and preferences

By default, all of this data is stored only on your device, in Rep Rat's private app storage. iOS protects that storage with its built-in data encryption whenever your device is locked with a passcode. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to enable Rep Rat Sync (see below).

2. Account and Rep Rat Sync

Rep Rat Sync is an optional paid feature. If you choose to enable it, you create an account using either your email address or Sign in with Apple. Your workout data is then synced to Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service operated by Google LLC.

We use your account solely to associate your data with your identity for sync purposes. We do not use your email for marketing.

If you do not create an account, no personal information is collected or transmitted by us.

Rep Rat Sync also offers optional cloud encryption. When it is turned on, your workout data is encrypted on your device with AES-256 before it is uploaded, using a key protected by a passphrase only you know. We cannot read encrypted workout data, and we cannot recover it if you forget your passphrase. Your account email and basic sync information, such as when a record was last updated, are not encrypted.

3. Third-Party Services

Rep Rat uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — cloud storage and authentication for Rep Rat Sync users. Data is stored in the United States. Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — processes in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect your device ID and purchase history. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Sign In — if you choose this login method, Apple handles authentication. Rep Rat only receives your Apple-assigned user ID and, on first sign-in, your name if you choose to share it.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or any other tracking services.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not track your location
  • We do not collect device identifiers for advertising
  • We do not share or sell your data to third parties
  • We do not use your data to train machine learning models

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your local data remains on your device until you delete the app or use the "Clear All Data" option in the app's settings.

If you have a Rep Rat Sync account, you can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time from the Profile tab (Profile → Delete Account). This removes your account and all of your cloud data, including anything synced, shared or posted from it. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

6. Data Export

You can export a full backup of your workout data in JSON format at any time from Settings in the app. You own your data.

7. Children's Privacy

Rep Rat is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@waddly.net.
